Frequently asked questions

Exploring a Partnership?

Does Farid Hospitality lease existing hotels?

Yes. We evaluate operational hotels, resorts and suitable hospitality properties for long-term lease arrangements. Rental terms are negotiated based on location, property condition, accommodation capacity and commercial potential.

Can I retain ownership while Farid Hospitality manages my hotel?

Yes. Our hotel management arrangements allow property owners to retain ownership while appointing Farid Hospitality to oversee day-to-day hotel operations under an agreed management structure.

Does Farid Hospitality acquire land for new hotels?

We consider land acquisition and development opportunities in selected tourism destinations. Proposals are evaluated based on location, accessibility, development feasibility and investment potential.

Are joint ventures with landowners possible?

Yes. We are open to evaluating joint development structures where landowners and Farid Hospitality can align their respective contributions and commercial interests.

How can I submit my hotel or land for consideration?

You can contact our business development team through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. Please include the property's location, size, current status and any available photographs or documents.
